Your Birth, Your Setting

Every environment, expertly supported

Hospital Support

Navigating a medical environment with confidence and calm.

  • Translating medical terminology and procedures
  • Protecting your birthing space and ambiance (lighting, sound)
  • Supporting epidural positioning for optimal fetal rotation
  • Advocating for your birth plan with hospital staff
  • Guiding partners on how to be involved in a clinical setting

Birth Center Support

Enhancing a physiological approach to birth.

  • Assisting with hydrotherapy and tub comfort
  • Continuous physical counter-pressure during active labor
  • Suggesting dynamic position changes
  • Maintaining hydration and energy levels
  • Collaborating seamlessly with midwives

Home Birth Support

Honoring the sanctuary of your own space.

  • Early labor support before the midwives arrive
  • Managing birth pool setup and logistics
  • Protecting the intimate family energy
  • Sibling integration and support if requested
  • Immediate postpartum cleanup and meal prep
