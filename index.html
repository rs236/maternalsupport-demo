This work is my calling, not just my career.

I believe every birthing person carries deep, ancestral wisdom within their body. In a medical landscape that can sometimes feel hurried and clinical, my role is to create a sanctuary of calm, evidence-based reassurance, and unbroken support.

How I Support You

From pregnancy through the fourth trimester

Continuous, evidence-based care structured around three foundational pillars.

Prenatal Preparation

Building confidence, physiological readiness, and clarity before labor begins.

  • 3 comprehensive prenatal visits (in-home)
  • Bespoke birth plan & advocacy preferences
  • Hands-on partner coaching & comfort techniques
  • Spinning Babies® pelvic alignment exercises

Continuous Labor Support

An unbroken, grounded presence from early labor through the golden hours.

  • 24/7 on-call availability starting at 37 weeks
  • Continuous physical, emotional & verbal comfort
  • Counter-pressure, rebozo sifting & position changes
  • Advocacy liaison with hospital/midwifery team

Fourth Trimester Sanctuary

Gentle transition support honoring the tender postpartum recovery window.

  • In-home postpartum follow-up & birth processing
  • Clinical lactation & feeding guidance
  • Newborn soothing, babywearing & swaddle coaching
  • Herbal sitz bath prep & recovery care plans
